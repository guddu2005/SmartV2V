## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from high-end neobanking and next-generation financial technology interfaces. The personality is defined by clean precision, effortless control, and modern minimalism. It balances hyper-refined visual hygiene with energetic moments of delight. 

Targeting digital natives, discerning professionals, and modern consumers who demand fluid utility, the UI evokes clarity, security, and progressive luxury. The aesthetic unifies tactile minimalism with high-end editorial restraint: ultra-crisp white floating surfaces, whisper-light borders, expansive negative space, deep obsidian contrast elements, and vibrant electric lime/neon green focal hits that communicate life, value, and momentum.

## Colors

The palette establishes a luminous, high-contrast hierarchy where content and actionable data shine without visual noise.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The root canvas sits on a gentle slate tint (`#f8fafc`), giving elevated cards (`#ffffff`) immediate spatial definition. Surface borders employ a delicate hairline tone (`#e2e8f0` or `#f1f5f9`) to anchor shapes softly against the background.
- **Primary & Contrasts:** Deep obsidian slate (`#0f172a`) acts as the anchor tone for primary call-to-action buttons, active navigation bars, primary headings, and high-impact structural blocks. Muted slate (`#64748b`) provides legible hierarchy for secondary labels, timestamps, and subtitles.
- **Vibrant Accents:** Electric neon lime (`#84cc16`) and rich emerald green (`#22c55e`) act as functional catalysts—used for card artworks, positive transactional gains, active toggle switches, and live telemetry indicators.
- **Priority & Status System:** 
  - *High Priority / Critical:* Soft pastel crimson wash (`#fef2f2`) with vivid crimson typography and stroke (`#e11d48`).
  - *Medium Priority / Pending:* Warm pastel amber tint (`#fffbeb`) grounded with burnt amber text (`#d97706`).
  - *Low / Neutral Status:* Crisp slate/gray wash (`#f1f5f9`) framed with cool slate copy (`#64748b`).
  - *Live Indicator:* Pure pulsing electric green (`#22c55e`) for real-time states and active accounts.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers the design system across all responsive tiers, delivering a balanced blend of geometric precision and humanist warmth. 

- **Headlines & Values:** Numerical metrics, balances, and primary titles leverage heavy weights (`700` and `600`) with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`), generating an editorial punch that mirrors physical premium payment cards.
- **Body & Captions:** Body typography balances readability with high breathing room, utilizing neutral weights (`400` and `500`) rendered in deep slate `#0f172a` for active copy and `#64748b` for metadata.
- **Labels & Micro-indicators:** Badges, timestamps, and button text utilize crisp, slightly tracked semi-bold and bold weights to retain razor-sharp legibility at petite physical sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous whitespace and modular vertical stacking, creating an unhurried, comfortable visual rhythm.

- **Grid Architecture:** Implements a fluid grid configured with 4 columns on mobile viewports (<640px), 8 columns on tablet devices (640px–1024px), and 12 columns across wide desktop displays (>1024px).
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely consistently on generous 16px to 24px (`space-md` to `space-lg`) internal gutters, ensuring transaction rows, settings toggles, and status blocks never feel cramped.
- **Stacking & Modular Flow:** Vertical card grouping utilizes 12px to 16px margins between interactive modules. Screen headers maintain a spacious offset from top navigation bezels, mimicking native mobile app status bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous surface separation rather than heavy, artificial drop shadows.

- **Ambient Elevation (Primary Cards):** Floating canvas elements sit on white base plates (`#ffffff`) accompanied by an ultra-soft dual-stage ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. This creates a weightless, floating tactile feel.
- **Micro Hairlines:** All cards and interactive rows are framed by a 1px solid hairline border (`#f1f5f9` or `#e2e8f0`). This ensures sharp structural delineation against the `#f8fafc` canvas under all monitor color profiles.
- **Interactive Depths:** Clickable cards and interactive list rows transition gently to a subtle lift state (`translateY(-1px)`) with shadow elevation amplifying slightly to `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`.
- **Floating Controls:** Primary bottom navigation bars and floating action trays utilize frosted glass diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a welcoming, hyper-modern rounded profile characterized by generous outer radii.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Large modules utilize `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` radii (18px to 24px), producing friendly silhouette boundaries seen in premium device hardware.
- **Buttons & Pills:** Primary and secondary action buttons adopt full pill profiles (`rounded-full` / 9999px) or disciplined 14px–16px radiused squares for compact icon trays.
- **Nested Inner Geometry:** Child elements (such as badge containers, avatar frames, and nested input inputs) scale proportionally down to 10px–12px radii, establishing harmonic corner-to-corner concentricity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Obsidian background (`#0f172a`), crisp white text (`#ffffff`), pill or 16px rounded profile, generous vertical padding (14px–16px), subtle inner highlight. Hover states ease into `#1e293b`.
- **Secondary / Action Tag:** High-contrast neutral tint (`#f1f5f9`), slate text (`#0f172a`), 0px border.
- **Accent Button:** Vibrant electric lime (`#84cc16`), deep obsidian label text (`#0f172a`), applied for card creation, top-ups, or prime conversions.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Rendered in solid pure white (`#ffffff`), 20px–24px corner radii, dual ambient shadows, and 1px border (`#f1f5f9`).
- Features dedicated card headers with 16px bold labels and inline ghost action links ("See all", "Edit").

### Priority Badges & Status Pills
- **High:** Tinted pastel crimson (`background: #fef2f2; color: #e11d48; border: 1px solid #fecdd3`).
- **Medium:** Tinted pastel amber (`background: #fffbeb; color: #d97706; border: 1px solid #fef3c7`).
- **Low / Neutral:** Crisp slate tint (`background: #f8fafc; color: #64748b; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0`).
- **Live Indicator Dot:** 6px–8px circular orb in glowing emerald green (`#22c55e`), paired with a faint ping animation or soft blur shadow.

### List Rows & Transaction Items
- Linear layout with leading circular/squircle icon avatars (44px, `#f8fafc` background with 1px border), clean bold transaction title (`#0f172a`), muted subtext timestamp (`#64748b`), and trailing formatted currency value.
- Positive gains display dynamic accent pills (`background: rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15); color: #4d7c0f`).

### Inputs & Toggles
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, 14px rounded borders, `#e2e8f0` border color, expanding focus ring in subtle obsidian tint (`0 0 0 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Toggles:** 48px width pill track. Inactive state is soft muted gray (`#e2e8f0`); active state fills with electric lime (`#84cc16`) with an ultra-white circular thumb (`#ffffff`) casting an ambient micro drop-shadow.

### Tab Navigation
- Floating bottom bar anchored on high-blur white acrylic, housing balanced mono-line SVG icons with active states signified by obsidian tint and micro electric lime indicator dots.
